# Operational & Logistics Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

**Microsoft Power BI | DAX | Interactive Business Intelligence & KPI Reporting**

---

## Project Overview

An interactive Power BI Business Intelligence dashboard built to monitor operational revenue, cost structure, net profitability, and regional delivery performance across business segments.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Business Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Total Revenue** | Tracks overall operational and commercial revenue volume |
| **Total Cost** | Monitors direct operational and transportation expenses |
| **Net Profit** | Evaluates net financial margin after operational costs |
| **Profit Margin (%)** | Measures profitability efficiency across cities and partners |

## Dashboard Features

1. **Monthly Revenue Dynamics:** Tracks revenue seasonality and month-over-month shifts.
2. **Partner & Channel Comparison:** Ranks top-performing corporate partners and service channels.
3. **Regional Profitability Breakdown:** Evaluates net profit across cities and operational modes.
4. **Dynamic Slicers:** Interactive filtering by Date Range, Customer Segment (*Individual, Corporate, Wholesale*), and Service Type.
