# Retail Inventory & Sales Performance Analytics (Excel)

**Microsoft Excel (Advanced Formulas, Pivot Tables, Dynamic Dashboards, KPI Tracking)**

---

## Project Overview

A comprehensive retail inventory and sales reporting workbook designed to monitor product movement, stock turnover, store-level sales performance, and revenue trends.

## Key Analytical Highlights

- **Sales & Revenue Tracking:** Dynamic breakdown of gross sales, units sold, and revenue contribution across product categories and sales points.
- **Inventory & Stock Control:** Monitoring stock levels, replenishment needs, and slow-moving vs. top-selling items.
- **Interactive Pivot Dashboards:** Executive summary tables and visual charts enabling rapid filtering by period, category, and location.
